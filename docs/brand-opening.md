# Brand opening

Verified October 23, 2026.

The opening uses example merchants listed on Rakuten. It does not claim that every merchant was a personal client, part of Programmatic Loyalty, or an endorser of Pranav. The original logo assets are rendered as monochrome silhouettes with preserved proportions, matching the portfolio palette.

## Visual direction

The October 23 revision removes the white cards and colored marks. A continuous field of monochrome logos magnifies from 0.68 to 1.6 scale with a restrained rotation, then dissolves into the portfolio. Foreground copy and dismissal controls remain stationary. The clipped decorative layer cannot create page overflow. Phone layouts use four columns, six rows and 24 marks. Each text block has its own compact opaque backdrop with soft edges. The surrounding story area stays transparent, with only a light radial shade, allowing brands to remain visible between and beside the text. Identity and navigation controls have the same protection at every viewport size. Smaller marks and a two-degree opening rotation keep the composition restrained.

## Behavior

- Plays on the first eligible visit and on browser reload, including hard refresh. Browser navigation timing does not distinguish ordinary reload from hard refresh.
- Lasts 3.6 seconds followed by a 0.3 second transition. Skip and Explore my work dismiss immediately.
- Does not replay on ordinary internal navigation, a return visit with saved state, history restoration, or a URL with an anchor.
- Reduced motion bypasses the opening. Changing that preference while it is open dismisses it.
- Native modal behavior contains keyboard focus. Escape dismisses it. Dismissal restores scrolling and moves focus to the page content.
- No audio, third-party asset requests, or video dependency. Failed logos fall back to merchant names.
- Without JavaScript or modal support, the underlying portfolio remains available.
- Phone widths show a smaller selection of logos to preserve legibility.
